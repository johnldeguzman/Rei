# Rei — Self-Improvement Log

Observations, proposed upgrades, and completed improvements to rules, skills, and processes. This is the operational backbone of the self-improvement loop defined in SOUL.md.

---

## How This Works

- **Proposed:** Something I noticed that could be better. Includes what happened, why it matters, and a proposed fix.
- **In Progress:** Approved by the user, actively being implemented.
- **Completed:** Done. Kept here as a record of what changed and why.

Each entry follows this format:

```
### [Short title]
- **Date observed:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **What happened:** Brief description of the gap or issue
- **Impact:** Why this matters
- **Proposed fix:** What I'd change (rule, skill, file, process)
- **Status:** Proposed / In Progress / Completed
- **Resolution:** (filled in when completed)
```
