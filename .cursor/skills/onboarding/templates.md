# Onboarding Plan Template

> **This is a starter template.** Replace the placeholder sections with your org's Confluence pages, Slack channels, team members, and onboarding milestones. The structure is generic; the content should be yours.

## Local Draft Template

```markdown
# Onboarding Plan - [NEW_PERSON_NAME]

| New Team Member | **[NEW_PERSON_NAME]** |
| --- | --- |
| Start Date | [START_DATE] |
| Buddy | @[BUDDY_NAME] |
| Manager | @[MANAGER_NAME] |

## Day 1: Welcome, get introduced

Welcome to the team! We're excited to have you on board.

- [ ] Get introduced in the team's Slack channel. Send a picture and a short bio to your manager or buddy.
- [ ] Update your Jira and Confluence profile with your title, picture, and contact info.
- [ ] Update your Slack profile with your title, picture, pronouns, and contact info.
- [ ] Join the team's Slack channels (listed below).
- [ ] Set up your development environment.
- [ ] Have a virtual hangout with your onboarding buddy.

| **Team channels** | [Add your team-specific Slack channels here] |
| --- | --- |
| **Functional channels** | [Add cross-functional channels here] |
| **Standard channels** | [Add org-wide channels: engineering, architecture, IT support, incidents, etc.] |
| **Just for fun** | [Add social/interest channels here] |

## Week 1: Getting acquainted with the team and codebase

- [ ] Read and understand the team's onboarding documentation. Bring questions to your buddy.
- [ ] Read and understand the team's architecture documentation.
- [ ] Read the team charter / mandate document.
- [ ] Walk through the relevant repos with your buddy or another developer.
- [ ] Read the employee handbook.
- [ ] Explore the product from a user's perspective.
- [ ] Pair program on 1 Jira ticket.
- [ ] Create your first pull request and release to production.
- [ ] Get familiar with your team's roadmap and the org-wide roadmaps.

## Month 1: Know your role and start contributing

- [ ] Schedule coffee chats with team members:
  - [ ] @[BUDDY_NAME]
  - [ ] [Add 2-3 team members here]

- [ ] Complete security and compliance training.
- [ ] Attend all onboarding sessions held by the People/HR team.
- [ ] Get familiar with the incident management process.
- [ ] Review the career framework / skills matrix with your manager.
- [ ] Understand your team's objectives and roadmap.
- [ ] Contribute to team deliverables at 50% capacity.
- [ ] Start shadowing the point-person / on-call rotation.
- [ ] Participate in the 30-day check-in with your manager.

## Month 2: Becoming impactful

- [ ] Schedule cross-team coffee chats:
  - [ ] [Add 3-4 people from adjacent teams here]

- [ ] Attend architecture reviews or design discussions.
- [ ] Sign up for technical onboarding sessions (if available).
- [ ] Contribute to team deliverables at 100% capacity.
- [ ] Join the point-person / on-call rotation.
- [ ] Consider employee referrals from your network.
- [ ] Participate in the 60-day check-in with your manager.

## Month 3: Developing your career

- [ ] Attend recurring engineering sync meetings.
- [ ] Contribute to an architecture review or ADR.
- [ ] Review a recent ADR your team has produced.
- [ ] Register for mentorship programs (if available).
- [ ] Update team goals and roadmap.
- [ ] Define career development goals with your manager.
- [ ] Participate in the 90-day check-in with your manager.
```
